# Mac Authenticator

Verification codes on the Mac for accounts also held in Google Authenticator.

## Language

**Account**: An authenticator entry containing a service name, account label and secret used to generate verification codes.

**Verification code**: A temporary number generated from an account's secret and the current time.

**Import**: Copying account entries from a QR export onto the Mac. It does not delete the iPhone entries or continuously synchronise account additions.

**Unlocked session**: The period after authenticating to this app and before locking the app, locking the Mac, sleeping, switching users or quitting.
